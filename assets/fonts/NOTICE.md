The Cairo family is bundled for offline rendering in Radar Labor.

- Designer: Mohamed Gaber, Accademia di Belle Arti di Urbino.
- Typeface source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cairo
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full license is in `OFL.txt`.
- The application uses the bundled variable font `Cairo-Variable.ttf` for
  Arabic and Latin text. It does not fetch fonts at runtime.
